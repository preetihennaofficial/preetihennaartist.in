Before you enrol

Questions worth asking first

Classes differ a lot from teacher to teacher. Ask these so you know exactly what the fee includes before you pay.

Send these questions on WhatsApp

  • Duration: how many sessions, how long each one is, and how often they run
  • Content: what each level covers, and what you should be able to do by the end
  • Materials: whether cones and practice sheets are included, or what to bring
  • Batch size: a group class or one-to-one
  • Timings and place: which days and times, and where classes are held
  • Certificate: whether one is given, if that matters to you
  • Payment: whether the fee is paid in full or in parts
