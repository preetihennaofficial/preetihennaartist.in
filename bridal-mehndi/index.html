What shapes a bridal quote

Two brides can ask for “bridal mehndi” and mean very different things. These are the details that move the price, so it helps to decide on them, or ask about them, early.

  • Coverage

    Palms and fingers, past the wrist, mid-forearm or up to the elbow, and whether the backs of the hands are included.

  • Feet

    Ask whether feet are part of your bridal design or quoted separately. Feet mehndi on its own starts at ₹500.

  • Design density

    An open, Arabic-influenced bridal design takes less time than dense traditional or Rajasthani-style fill.

  • Personal details

    Names, initials or a date worked into the design. Customized name or text mehndi starts at ₹200.

  • Embellishment

    Glitter or stone work added for photographs and the ceremony starts at ₹150.

  • Family and guests

    If relatives or guests also need mehndi, give the number of people so it can all be quoted together.

  • Date, timing and place

    Wedding-season dates, early starts and where the mehndi is applied all need to be agreed in advance.

Planning the timing

Henna paste needs time to dry on the skin, and the stain usually deepens over the following day or two. That is why many brides schedule their mehndi one or two days before the wedding, often as part of the mehndi ceremony.

Bridal designs take hours rather than minutes. Ask how long your design will take so the rest of the day can be planned around it.

For wedding-season dates, enquire as early as you can. Your date is held once Preeti confirms it and half the amount is paid in advance.

Engagement mehndi

Engagement mehndi starts at ₹1,500. It is usually lighter than bridal work, often on the hands only and with less coverage, and suits a ring ceremony or roka. Share a reference photo and the coverage you want for a quote.

What to send with your enquiry

  • Your mehndi date and your wedding date
  • Where you would like the mehndi applied
  • The coverage you want, using the terms above
  • Two or three reference photos of designs you like
  • Any names, initials or dates to include
  • How many family members or guests also need mehndi
  • Any skin sensitivity or past reaction to henna

Before you book

A few things to settle with Preeti on WhatsApp before the day:

  • The final price and exactly what it covers: both hands, front and back, how far up the arm, feet
  • The advance: half the amount is paid when you book, to hold your date
  • The start time and roughly how long it will take
  • The place: at the shop, or at your home or venue in Faridabad or Delhi, with travel costs covered by you
  • Aftercare instructions for looking after the design afterwards

Questions

Bridal mehndi questions

Is ₹5,100 the full price of bridal mehndi?

It is the starting price. The final figure depends on coverage, design density, feet, personal details and add-ons such as glitter. Preeti confirms the exact price after seeing your references.

Can I show a design I found online?

Share reference photos on WhatsApp when you enquire. Preeti can tell you how closely a design can be followed and how it affects the quote.

Can names or initials be hidden in the design?

Customized name or text mehndi starts at ₹200. Send the names or initials with your enquiry and ask how they are priced within your bridal design.

How dark will my mehndi be?

No artist can promise an exact shade. Stain colour varies from person to person and depends on skin, body warmth, how long the paste stays on, and aftercare. Ask Preeti for aftercare instructions at your appointment.

Can mehndi be applied at home or at the venue?

Yes. For bridal mehndi and functions, Preeti comes to your home or venue in Faridabad or Delhi, and travel costs are covered by you. You are also welcome at the shop in Sector 91.

I have sensitive skin. What should I do?

Mention it in your enquiry and ask what the mehndi cone contains. If you have reacted to henna before, talk to Preeti first and consider a patch test well before the wedding.
