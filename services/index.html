Styles explained

How the styles differ

Style names are used loosely, so a reference photo is still the clearest way to show what you want.

Arabic

Bold flowers, leaves and vines, usually trailing diagonally across the back of the hand or along one side of the palm. Open skin between the motifs makes the design read clearly. Quicker to apply than dense styles.

Indian traditional

Symmetrical, closely filled patterns such as a central mandala, mesh-like jaal, paisleys (keri), peacocks and lotus motifs, often covering most of the hand and fingers.

Rajasthani

A traditional style known for very fine lines and intricate, often figurative, detail with little empty space. It takes longer to apply, which is reflected in the quote.

Questions

Pricing questions

Are these prices per hand or per person?

The prices are starting prices for each service. They are not stated per hand or per person, so ask what your quote includes: one hand or both, front only or front and back.

Why could my quote be higher than the starting price?

Denser designs, more coverage, feet, names and glitter or stone work all add time. The date and timing you need can also affect the quote.

Can you quote for a group or an event?

Yes. Send the number of people, the date, the place and the kind of designs you have in mind. Simple guest designs start from ₹100 each; the total depends on the number of guests and designs.

Is the price confirmed before the mehndi is applied?

Agree the final price with Preeti on WhatsApp before your appointment, together with what it covers. That way there are no surprises on the day.
