Choosing a style

Three styles, three different looks

Not sure what to ask for? Most enquiries fall into one of these. A reference photo of a design you like settles the rest.

Arabic

Bold, flowing flowers and leaves that usually trail diagonally across the hand, with open skin between the motifs. Quicker to apply and easy to read from a distance.

Often chosen for parties, guests and festivals. From ₹150.

Indian traditional & Rajasthani

Dense, symmetrical fine-line work (mandalas, mesh-like jaal, paisleys and peacocks) that fills most of the hand. Rajasthani designs are known for especially fine lines and figurative detail.

Often chosen for weddings, Karwa Chauth and family functions. From ₹200.

Bridal

The most detailed work, usually covering both sides of the hands and well up the arms, often with feet, and with room for personal touches such as names or initials. Coverage is agreed with you before the quote.

For the bride. From ₹5,100. Bridal details

How booking works
